Hide and Clone: Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026. This policy covers the game Hide and Clone on the web and on Windows. The game is published by 20Vision GmbH, Anisgasse 30, 1220 Vienna, Austria.

No accounts

The game has no accounts, no purchases and no third-party advertising. It does mention and link to our own product, MyClawn. You do not give us an email address or a password.

Usage statistics

We collect anonymous usage statistics without cookies (daily-rotating hash, no IP storage). The game has no logins, so no usage is linked to an account. The statistics cover game events such as opening the game, hosting or joining a game, rounds started and which platform is in use, along with device type, operating system, screen size, language and approximate location (country, region, city). Your IP address is used only for a moment to create the daily hash and the approximate location, and it is not stored.

Multiplayer

Multiplayer is player-hosted. Our lobby service lists open games and connects players. It handles your connection address, your game's name, map name and player counts while you use it, and it does not keep them after the game closes. Game traffic then runs directly between players, so other players in your game can technically see your IP address. To set up that direct connection, the game contacts public STUN servers operated by Google, which see your IP address.

The player name you choose is shown to other players in your game. Do not use your real name if you don't want others to see it.

Stored on your device

The game stores your player name and the maps you build on your device. They stay there until you remove the game or its data. Maps you play with others are sent to the players in that game.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell data and do not share it for advertising. The statistics stay on our own analytics system, hosted with our infrastructure providers. Anonymous statistics may be kept for as long as they are useful to us for improving the game.

Your rights

If you are in the EU, you can ask us about access, correction, deletion or objection, and you can complain to the Austrian data protection authority. Because the statistics are anonymous, we usually cannot link them to you.
