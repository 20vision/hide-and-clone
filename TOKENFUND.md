# Hide & Clone
Vision: Free hide and seek with cute glass clones: build a map, hit Create, and invite friends.
Image: store/listing/2_hide.png
Chat: https://discord.gg/spUZ8ruSDJ

A browser game where the host's browser runs the match over WebRTC. server/lobby.mjs only lists lobbies and connects players, and it must stay that small: no paid central servers.

Good first work:
- New game modes and map ideas.
- Better touch controls on phones.
- Bugs in networked rounds (tools/test_web.mjs plays a full round).

A good change is small and keeps the game free.
